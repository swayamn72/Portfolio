Community guidelines

Last updated 26 November 2026.

TURFR works when everyone feels safe on the road and on the map. These rules apply to your username, display name, club names and descriptions, challenge names, and how you treat others.

Be decent

  • No harassment, threats, bullying or hate based on who someone is.
  • No sexual, violent or shocking names or content.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, or a brand or official body.
  • Don't post anyone's personal details, such as a phone number or where they live.
  • No spam, advertising, or anything illegal in India.

Play fair

  • Run your own runs, on foot. No faked locations, vehicles or shared accounts.
  • Don't follow, wait for, or track other runners because of what you see on the map.

Report and block

Use Report on a runner (in Friends, on their profile, or in a club) or on a club to tell us about a problem, and Block to stop a runner seeing or reaching you. The other person isn't told you blocked them. We review reports and may warn, hide content, or suspend accounts. You can appeal a decision in the app.

Contact and grievance officer
