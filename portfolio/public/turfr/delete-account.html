Delete your TURFR account

TURFR is an app by Swayam Nakte. Last updated 26 November 2026.

You can delete your TURFR account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TURFR and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Under Data and account, tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete my account permanently.

You're signed out on every device straight away, and your data is erased within minutes.

Want a copy first? In the same place, tap Export your data.

Without the app

Email swayamsn123@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my TURFR account.

We only act on a request sent from the account's own email address, so nobody else can delete your account. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some of your data, keep your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove data:

  • A run: open the run, tap Delete run, then Yes, delete it. Its GPS points, route, distance, times and stats are deleted at once. We keep only a note that a deleted run existed, so it can't be uploaded twice. XP and turf you already earned from it stay on your account.
  • A privacy zone: in Profile → Settings, open your privacy zones and remove it.
  • Your display name or home area: edit or clear them in your profile.
  • Anything else: email swayamsn123@gmail.com from your sign-up address saying what you want removed. We do it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Raw GPS points from every run are deleted automatically after 30 days, even if you do nothing. The trimmed route and run summary stay until you delete the run or your account.

What is deleted with your account

  • Your account: email, username, display name, home area and password.
  • Your runs: routes, GPS points, distances, times and run history.
  • Your game progress: turf, XP, badges, quests and leaderboard places.
  • Your friends, blocks, safety contacts, privacy zones and settings.
  • Your sign-in sessions, devices and notification choices.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A record that an account was deleted and when (no name, email or runs), kept to show we honoured the request and to prevent abuse.
  • If you had opted out of our emails, or they bounced, your address stays on a do-not-email list so we never email you again.
  • Totals that can't identify you, such as overall run counts.

Your date of birth is never stored: it is only used at sign-up to check that you are 18 or older.
