Privacy notice

Last updated 26 November 2026.

This notice explains what TURFR collects when you use the app, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how you control it. TURFR is for adults (18 or older) in India.

What we collect and why

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash), your username, display name, chosen avatar and, if you pick one, your home area (a neighbourhood name, never an address). Used to run your account and let friends find you.
  • Age check: at sign-up you enter your date of birth so we can check you are 18 or older. We check it and then discard it; we keep only the fact that the check passed.
  • Runs: while you are recording a run, your phone's precise location (GPS points with times). We use it to measure your distance and time, check the run is genuine, and work out the ground you claim in Turf. We record only while a run is in progress, never in the background otherwise.
  • Privacy zones: places you mark (for example home). We cut the part of every run near them on our servers before anything is saved or shown.
  • Run verification: if you choose to verify a run, your phone asks for your screen lock. The check happens on your phone; we only learn whether it passed.
  • Social and game activity: friends, clubs, challenges, blocks, reports you make, territory claims, XP and achievements.
  • Safety contacts: the email address of anyone you add as a safety contact, and whether they accepted.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, and your notification and email choices.
  • Technical records: request logs with your IP address and device type, kept to run and protect the service. They never contain your location or sign-in codes.

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, we don't sell your data, and we don't collect photos, contacts from your address book, or health data such as heart rate.

What other people can see

  • Your profile (username, display name, avatar, home area if you set one) is visible to everyone and findable by username unless you change it: Profile → Settings → Privacy & safety → Privacy & visibility lets you choose Friends or Only me, and turn off search.
  • Your runs: your friends see each run that counts, with its distance, time, pace, route (including where it starts and finishes) and the day, but not the time of day. You can change who sees them (Everyone, Friends or Only me), hide route maps, show start times, or turn on Hide start and finish to cut the first and last 200 m of every route, in the same privacy settings. The parts of a run near your privacy zones are always cut before anyone else sees it. Runs that didn't count are never shown.
  • Your claimed ground appears on the Turf map, with your name unless you turn that off in the same privacy settings.
  • Your friends see your weekly totals on their friend board. Turn off Friend board in the same privacy settings to leave it.
  • Other runners can race a trimmed copy of a loop you ran (Ghost Races). It never shows where you started. Turn off Allow Ghost Races on my loops in the same privacy settings.
  • Global leaderboards are off until you turn them on, and show only scores.
  • Club members see club results, never your route or location.
  • If you share a run with a safety contact, they see a rough area (at least 500 m across), at least 15 minutes late, and only until the run ends or you stop sharing. TURFR is not an emergency service.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers who process data only on our instructions: a cloud hosting provider that runs our servers and database, an email delivery provider that sends account and safety emails, and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications (which receives a device token and a notification id, never your location). Map images load straight from OpenFreeMap, which sees your IP address and the part of the map you are looking at. We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Raw GPS points from a run: 30 days, then deleted. The trimmed route and run summary stay until you delete the run or your account.
  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete your account. Deletion signs you out everywhere and erases your data within minutes.
  • After deletion we keep only a minimal record that an account was deleted (to prevent abuse), email addresses that bounced or complained (so we never email them again), and staff audit records that no longer identify you.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Settings → Data and account → Export your data. It covers your account, settings, privacy zones, runs, ground, XP, badges, friends, blocks, safety contacts, reports you made, notification choices and what you agreed to.
  • Correct it: edit your profile, username and settings in the app.
  • Delete it: open a run and tap Delete run to delete that run, or delete your whole account in the app (Profile → Settings → Data and account → Delete account), or by writing from your account's email address to swayamsn123@gmail.com, which erases all your runs too.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off location for TURFR in Android settings (you can still browse), turn off leaderboards, safety sharing and news emails in the app, or delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't undo what was done before.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act: write to the grievance officer.
  • Complain: to our grievance officer first. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How we protect it

Runs waiting to upload are stored encrypted on your phone. Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, staff access is limited by role and logged, and sign-in codes and locations are kept out of our logs. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes

When this notice changes we will tell you in the app and ask you to agree before you carry on.
